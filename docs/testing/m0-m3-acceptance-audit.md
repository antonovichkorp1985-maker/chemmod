### 2026-10-25 continuation — prepared copper coupon implementation (verification pending)

## Evidence and gaps

### M0 — CLI core

- `Molecule`/`MoleculeGraph`, `SmilesLikeParser`, `MoleculeCanonicalizer`, `Validation`, `PropertyPredictor` and `core-cli` provide the named source features.
- `MoleculeTest` checks graph validity, formula/molar mass and distinguishing ethanol from dimethyl ether. The earlier assertion of **83.7 °C** is replaced by the graph-family predictor; the current estimate is **78.1 °C**. GitHub CI runs `40421266367` and `40421272534` passed on `7b5ccd4`, including the C6 holdout/refusal fixtures and all 28 boiling-model tests. The final owner review is scheduled after M0–M4 implementation is complete.
- The plan's M0 row states “F1 (ethanol ≈64 °C)”. The owner clarified that properties must follow reality through general models/rules: 64 °C is near methanol, while measured ethanol is around 78.3–78.4 °C. Do not accept the former 83.7 °C output by setting a broad tolerance; do not add an ethanol-only calibration. The generic family fit predicts about 78.1 °C, but ethanol was used in fitting. Independent NIST checks found 1.4–9.6 K errors for OH-substituted C5–C6 isomers under the old formula. A graph-derived OH-arm term, fit on 2-/3-pentanol and checked on 2-/3-hexanol, gives +0.37 K residuals on that C6 pair. An OH-to-branch-distance term is fit on C4/C5 primary alcohols and checked on proximal C6 2-methyl-1-pentanol (+0.29 K). NIST 3-/4-methyl-1-pentanol have conflicting position residuals under the binary distal descriptor, so distal-primary C6 is now refused; branched-secondary and tertiary C5–C6 also remain refused. GitHub CI `40421272534` passed the current fixtures; the small holdouts support a limited domain rather than broad accuracy. The integrated owner review is scheduled after M0–M4 implementation is complete.
